# Board Meeting Notes — April 16, 2026

**Location:** Company HQ, 4th Floor Conference Room / Zoom
**Time:** 10:00 AM – 1:30 PM PT
**Chair:** Michael Torres, Board Chair

## Attendees

**Board Members Present:** Michael Torres (Chair), Angela Reeves, Dr. James Whitfield, Priya Kapoor, Robert Nakamura
**Board Members Absent:** Sandra Liu (excused — travel conflict)
**Management Present:** CEO Alex Drummond, CFO Sarah Chen, CTO David Park, VP Product Marcus Rivera, VP Sales Tom Bradley, General Counsel Nina Ochoa

## Opening Remarks

Michael Torres called the meeting to order at 10:05 AM. Quorum was confirmed with five of six directors present. Minutes from the February 2026 board meeting were approved unanimously without amendment.

## CEO Update

Alex Drummond presented the Q1 business review. Key points:

- Revenue of $14.7M against $15.2M target; shortfall attributed to two slipped Enterprise deals expected to close in April
- ARR reached $58.8M, up 22% year-over-year
- Headcount grew to 142 employees (from 128 at year-end 2025)
- Customer count: 287 active accounts; NPS reached 58, a company record
- The AI Assistant beta was well-received, with 47 participating customers reporting a 35% reduction in support tickets

Priya Kapoor asked about competitive dynamics. Alex noted that two competitors launched AI features in Q1, but our differentiation on enterprise security and auditability remains strong. The sales win rate against the top competitor held steady at 62%.

## Financial Review

Sarah Chen presented the detailed financial review:

- Gross margin: 74.2%, above the 73% target
- Operating expenses: $13.1M (89% of revenue), within budget
- Cash position: $28.4M with 22 months of runway at current burn
- The company is on track for cash-flow breakeven by Q1 2027

Robert Nakamura requested a sensitivity analysis on the path to breakeven under different growth scenarios. Sarah agreed to prepare this for the June meeting.

## Product and Engineering Update

Marcus Rivera and David Park jointly presented the product roadmap and engineering capacity:

- API v3 is on track for April 30 delivery
- AI Assistant GA moved from May to June 15 to incorporate beta feedback
- FedRAMP readiness assessment engagement with the 3PAO is confirmed for September
- Engineering team at 64 engineers with 22 additional hires planned for 2026

Angela Reeves asked about the talent market. David noted that offer acceptance rates improved to 78% in Q1, up from 65% in 2025, attributed to the enhanced equity refresh program.

## Closed Session

The board entered closed session at 12:45 PM to discuss the Series C financing timeline with Nina Ochoa. Management (except CEO) was excused. The board authorized the CEO to engage two investment banks for a formal process, targeting a $50M–$75M raise at a $400M–$500M pre-money valuation. Term sheet target: September 2026.

## Action Items

1. CFO to prepare breakeven sensitivity analysis for June board meeting
2. CEO to engage investment banks for Series C process by May 1
3. General Counsel to prepare updated board consent for Series C authorization
4. VP Product to present competitive analysis deep-dive at June meeting

Meeting adjourned at 1:25 PM. Next board meeting: June 18, 2026.

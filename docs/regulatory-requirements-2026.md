# Regulatory Requirements Overview — 2026

**Prepared by:** Nina Ochoa, General Counsel
**Date:** March 25, 2026
**Distribution:** Executive Team, Compliance, Engineering Leadership

## Purpose

This document provides a comprehensive overview of regulatory requirements affecting our business in 2026. The regulatory landscape has expanded significantly, with new frameworks in AI governance, data privacy, and financial services requiring coordinated responses across legal, engineering, and compliance teams.

## EU AI Act — High-Risk AI System Requirements

The EU AI Act provisions for high-risk AI systems take effect August 1, 2026. Our AI Assistant product falls under the "limited risk" category based on our current assessment, but customers deploying it for automated decision-making in healthcare or financial services may elevate it to "high risk" in their context.

Required actions before the compliance deadline of July 15, 2026:

- Complete the AI system risk classification for all AI-powered features
- Implement transparency requirements: users must be informed when interacting with AI-generated content
- Establish the AI quality management system documentation
- Conduct and document the conformity assessment for the AI Assistant
- Designate an EU AI Act compliance officer (proposed: Rachel Kim, Chief Privacy Officer)

## Data Privacy Regulations

### CPRA Enforcement Updates

The California Privacy Protection Agency issued updated enforcement guidelines in January 2026, expanding the definition of "sensitive personal information" and strengthening opt-out requirements for cross-context behavioral advertising. Our legal team has reviewed the guidelines and identified two areas requiring product changes:

- The privacy preference center must support Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals by June 1, 2026
- Data broker registration requirements may apply to our analytics partnerships — legal review in progress

### EU Data Governance Act

The Data Governance Act's provisions on data intermediary services became applicable in September 2025. While our primary operations are not classified as data intermediary services, our marketplace feature may require notification to the relevant national authority. Legal analysis is pending.

## Financial Services — SOX Readiness

As the company approaches a potential IPO timeline, SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley) readiness is becoming relevant. While not currently required, the CFO and General Counsel recommend beginning preparatory work in H2 2026:

- Engage an external advisor for SOX readiness assessment ($150K–$200K budget)
- Begin documenting internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR)
- Implement segregation of duties in financial systems
- Evaluate ERP system upgrades to support SOX-compliant audit trails

## FedRAMP Authorization

The FedRAMP Moderate authorization process is a 2026 priority aligned with the product roadmap. The compliance deadline for submitting the System Security Plan (SSP) to the FedRAMP PMO is October 31, 2026. Key milestones:

- 3PAO engagement: confirmed (September 2026 readiness assessment)
- GovCloud environment buildout: Q3 2026 (Engineering)
- Security control implementation: 325 of 421 controls documented, 96 remaining
- Continuous monitoring infrastructure: design complete, implementation Q3

## Coordination and Governance

A cross-functional Regulatory Coordination Committee has been established, meeting bi-weekly, with representation from Legal, Compliance, Engineering, Product, and Finance. The committee maintains a shared regulatory calendar and escalation process. Quarterly updates will be provided to the board of directors.

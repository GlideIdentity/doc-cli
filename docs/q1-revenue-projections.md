# Q1 2026 Revenue Projections

**Prepared by:** Sarah Chen, VP of Finance
**Date:** April 2, 2026
**Status:** Final

## Executive Summary

Q1 2026 closed with total revenue of $14.7M against a target of $15.2M, representing a 3.3% shortfall. Despite headwinds in the Enterprise segment, strong performance in Mid-Market and Self-Serve channels partially offset the gap. Recurring revenue grew 18% year-over-year, and net revenue retention improved to 112%.

## Revenue by Segment

| Segment | Target | Actual | Variance |
|---------|--------|--------|----------|
| Enterprise | $8.5M | $7.8M | -$700K |
| Mid-Market | $4.2M | $4.5M | +$300K |
| Self-Serve | $1.8M | $1.9M | +$100K |
| Professional Services | $0.7M | $0.5M | -$200K |
| **Total** | **$15.2M** | **$14.7M** | **-$500K** |

Enterprise underperformance was driven by two delayed deals — Meridian Financial ($420K ACV) and TechBridge Solutions ($310K ACV) — both of which slipped into Q2 due to extended procurement cycles. The sales team has confirmed both are in final contract review and expected to close by mid-April.

Mid-Market exceeded targets for the third consecutive quarter, largely attributable to the new vertical-specific packaging launched in November 2025. The healthcare and fintech bundles accounted for 62% of new Mid-Market bookings.

## Key Metrics

- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR):** $4.9M (up from $4.1M in Q1 2025)
- **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR):** $58.8M
- **Net Revenue Retention:** 112% (target: 110%)
- **Gross Margin:** 74.2% (target: 73%)
- **Average Contract Value (Enterprise):** $187K (up 14% YoY)
- **Sales Cycle Length:** 68 days (down from 74 days in Q4 2025)

## Q2 Outlook and Adjustments

Based on current pipeline and the two slipped Enterprise deals, we are maintaining the Q2 target of $16.1M. The pipeline coverage ratio stands at 3.4x, above our 3.0x threshold. Key risks include potential macroeconomic softening in the financial services vertical and the upcoming pricing migration for legacy customers, which may temporarily increase churn in May.

We recommend accelerating the Mid-Market hiring plan by one month and investing an additional $150K in demand generation for the healthcare vertical, where conversion rates are 40% above average. Finance will present updated full-year projections at the May board meeting incorporating these adjustments.

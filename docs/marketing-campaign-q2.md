# Q2 2026 Marketing Campaign Plan

**Owner:** Jennifer Walsh, VP of Marketing
**Date:** March 20, 2026
**Budget Period:** April 1 – June 30, 2026

## Campaign Overview

Q2 marketing efforts will focus on three strategic campaigns: the AI Assistant product launch, the Healthcare Vertical Expansion initiative, and the annual user conference (Elevate 2026). Combined, these campaigns target 2,400 marketing-qualified leads (MQLs) and $3.8M in influenced pipeline.

## Campaign 1: AI Assistant Launch — "Work Smarter"

**Timeline:** April 14 – May 31, 2026
**Budget:** $320K
**Target MQLs:** 900

The AI Assistant general availability launch is our flagship Q2 initiative. The campaign will run across digital channels with a focus on demonstrating ROI through customer case studies and interactive demos.

Tactics:
- Launch event webinar featuring 3 beta customers (April 14, target: 800 registrants)
- Paid search campaign targeting "AI workflow automation" and related keywords ($85K)
- LinkedIn sponsored content series — 6 posts over 8 weeks ($45K)
- Product Hunt launch (April 15)
- Analyst briefings with Gartner and Forrester (pre-launch, March 28–April 10)
- Content series: 4 blog posts, 2 whitepapers, 1 ROI calculator
- Email nurture sequence to existing prospects (4,200 contacts)

## Campaign 2: Healthcare Vertical — "Trusted by Care Teams"

**Timeline:** May 1 – June 30, 2026
**Budget:** $180K
**Target MQLs:** 600

Building on strong Q1 conversion rates in healthcare, this campaign targets hospital systems, telehealth providers, and health tech companies.

Tactics:
- Sponsorship at HIMSS Digital Health Summit ($60K, includes booth and speaking slot)
- Co-branded case study with NovaCare Health System
- Healthcare-specific landing page with HIPAA compliance messaging
- Targeted ABM campaign reaching 150 healthcare IT decision-makers ($40K)
- Partnership webinar with Epic Systems integration team

## Campaign 3: Elevate 2026 User Conference

**Timeline:** June 17–18, 2026 (San Francisco)
**Budget:** $450K
**Target Attendees:** 600

Our second annual user conference brings together customers, prospects, and partners for keynotes, product deep-dives, and networking.

Key details:
- Venue: Moscone West, San Francisco
- Keynote speakers: CEO, VP Product, plus 2 customer executives (confirmed: CTO of Meridian Financial, VP Ops of CloudFirst)
- 18 breakout sessions across 3 tracks (Product, Technical, Strategy)
- Partner expo with 12 exhibitors (sponsorship revenue: $180K)
- Early-bird registration opens April 7

# Customer Success Metrics — Q1 2026

**Prepared by:** Lisa Tran, Director of Customer Success
**Date:** April 5, 2026
**Distribution:** Executive Team, Board of Directors

## Overview

The Customer Success team achieved strong results in Q1 2026, with improvements across all primary KPIs. Net Promoter Score reached its highest level in company history, and churn rate declined for the fourth consecutive quarter. The team managed 287 active accounts across Enterprise and Mid-Market segments.

## Churn Analysis

Seven accounts churned in Q1, down from eleven in Q4 2025. Total churned ARR was $312K. Exit interviews and analysis identified the following primary reasons:

- **Budget cuts / economic pressure:** 3 accounts ($145K ARR)
- **Switched to competitor:** 2 accounts ($98K ARR) — both cited missing workflow automation features that are on the 2026 roadmap
- **Acquired / merged:** 1 account ($42K ARR)
- **Product fit:** 1 account ($27K ARR) — early-stage startup that outgrew our SMB tier

## Customer Health Trends

We implemented a revised health scoring model in January that incorporates product usage telemetry, support sentiment analysis, and executive engagement frequency. The new model correctly predicted 5 of 7 churned accounts as "at risk" at least 45 days before cancellation, compared to 3 of 11 under the previous model.

Accounts currently flagged as at-risk: 14 (representing $890K ARR). The CS team has initiated intervention playbooks for all flagged accounts, including executive business reviews, custom training sessions, and product feedback loops.

## Q2 Priorities

1. Launch the Customer Advisory Board with 15 strategic accounts to drive product feedback and deepen executive relationships
2. Pilot the automated onboarding program for Self-Serve tier to reduce Time to First Value below 7 days
3. Expand the CS team by two Customer Success Managers to maintain a ratio below 40 accounts per CSM
4. Implement quarterly business reviews for all accounts above $100K ARR

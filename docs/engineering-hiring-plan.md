# Engineering Hiring Plan — 2026

**Prepared by:** David Park, VP of Engineering
**Date:** March 15, 2026
**Approved by:** Executive Leadership Team

## Current State

The engineering organization currently has 64 full-time engineers across six teams: Platform (12), Frontend (10), Backend Services (14), Data Engineering (8), Infrastructure (10), and Security (6). We also have 4 engineering managers and 2 directors. Current attrition is running at 8% annually, below the industry average of 13%.

## Hiring Targets

We plan to add 22 net-new engineers in 2026 to support the product roadmap and reduce team load to sustainable levels. The Infrastructure and Security teams are currently operating above capacity, with both teams reporting sustained sprint velocity issues.

### Q3–Q4 Hires (8 positions)

The remaining 8 positions are budgeted for Q3–Q4 and will be allocated based on roadmap priorities reviewed at the mid-year planning cycle. Tentative allocation includes 3 additional Backend Services engineers for the Workflow Builder 2.0 project, 2 Infrastructure engineers for FedRAMP, and 3 positions to be determined.

## Compensation and Budget

Total incremental compensation cost for 22 hires is estimated at $5.8M annually (loaded cost including benefits, equipment, and onboarding). The average offer package for L5 engineers is $245K total compensation (base + equity + bonus). We remain competitive at the 65th percentile of the Bay Area market for senior roles and 75th percentile for staff-level positions.

## Recruiting Capacity

Our internal recruiting team can support approximately 3–4 hires per month. For the Q1–Q2 ramp, we have engaged two external recruiting firms — TalentBridge Partners and Apex Technical Search — under contingency agreements at 20% of first-year base salary. Estimated external recruiting fees: $480K.

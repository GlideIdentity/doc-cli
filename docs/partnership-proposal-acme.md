# Partnership Proposal — ACME Corp

**Prepared by:** Tom Bradley, VP of Sales & Partnerships
**Date:** April 12, 2026
**Status:** Draft — Pending Executive Review
**Confidentiality:** Business Confidential

## Executive Summary

We propose a strategic technology partnership with ACME Corp to integrate our platform with ACME's enterprise resource planning (ERP) suite, serving their 3,200+ mid-market and enterprise customers. The partnership would include a native integration, co-selling arrangement, and joint go-to-market campaign. We estimate this partnership could generate $2.8M in influenced revenue in the first 12 months and establish a durable channel that scales with ACME's customer base.

## About ACME Corp

ACME Corp is a publicly traded (NYSE: ACME) enterprise software company headquartered in Austin, TX, with $1.2B in annual revenue. Their ERP platform serves 3,200 customers across manufacturing, logistics, and professional services. Key stats:

- Founded: 2008
- Employees: 4,800
- Customer base: predominantly mid-market ($50M–$500M revenue companies)
- Platform: cloud-native, API-first architecture
- Marketplace: 180+ third-party integrations

## Partnership Structure

### Technical Integration

We propose building a bidirectional integration between our workflow automation platform and ACME's ERP suite:

- **Data sync:** Real-time synchronization of customer records, orders, and inventory data
- **Workflow triggers:** ACME ERP events (order placed, shipment delayed, invoice overdue) trigger automated workflows in our platform
- **Embedded experience:** Our workflow builder embedded within the ACME dashboard via iframe/SDK
- **Authentication:** SSO via SAML 2.0 with ACME's identity provider

Engineering estimate: 3 engineers for 10 weeks (1 from ACME, 2 from our team). ACME has confirmed willingness to dedicate engineering resources pending partnership approval.

### Commercial Terms

- **Revenue share:** 20% referral fee on net-new customers originating from ACME's marketplace or sales team, paid quarterly in arrears for the first 12 months of each customer's contract
- **Co-selling:** Joint account mapping with ACME's sales team, targeting 50 shared prospects in Q3 2026
- **Marketplace listing:** Featured placement in ACME's integration marketplace for the first 6 months post-launch
- **Minimum commitment:** Neither party commits to revenue minimums; the arrangement is performance-based

### Go-to-Market Plan

- **Launch webinar:** Joint webinar targeting ACME's customer base (target: 500 registrants)
- **Case study:** Develop a joint case study with a mutual customer (candidate: Pacific Manufacturing Group)
- **Conference presence:** Joint booth at ACME's annual user conference (ACMEcon, September 2026)
- **Content:** 3 co-branded blog posts, 1 solution brief, 1 integration guide

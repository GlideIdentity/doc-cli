# Data Privacy Policy Update — May 2026

**Author:** Rachel Kim, Chief Privacy Officer
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2026
**Review Cycle:** Annual (next review: June 2027)

## Purpose

This document outlines updates to the company's data privacy policy in response to evolving regulatory requirements, including the EU AI Act provisions taking effect in August 2026, the updated California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) enforcement guidelines, and internal improvements based on the Q1 compliance audit findings.

All employees, contractors, and third-party processors handling personal data must review and acknowledge these updates before the compliance deadline of May 25, 2026.

## Key Policy Changes

### 1. AI System Data Processing

With the general availability of our AI Assistant, we are introducing specific provisions for AI-related data processing:

- All training data used for AI model fine-tuning must be reviewed and approved by the Data Governance Committee
- Customer data is never used for model training unless the customer provides explicit, documented opt-in consent
- AI-generated outputs that contain or reference personal data must be logged in the AI audit trail with a 12-month retention period
- Customers may request a complete export of all AI interactions involving their data through the existing DSAR (Data Subject Access Request) process

### 2. Cross-Border Data Transfer Updates

Following the adoption of the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework extension, we are updating our Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs):

- All new SCCs must use the June 2025 European Commission template
- Transfer Impact Assessments are now required for any new data flow to jurisdictions not covered by an adequacy decision
- The legal team has completed updated TIAs for our AWS regions in US-East, US-West, EU-Frankfurt, and AP-Singapore
- Data transfers to sub-processors must be documented in the vendor data flow registry within 5 business days of contract execution

### 3. Enhanced Data Subject Rights

We are expanding our data subject rights capabilities to include:

- Automated processing of deletion requests within 72 hours (reduced from 30 days)
- Machine-readable data portability exports in JSON and CSV formats
- Granular consent management allowing data subjects to opt in or out of specific processing activities
- A new self-service privacy dashboard accessible from the account settings page

### 4. Vendor and Sub-Processor Management

Third-party risk assessment requirements have been strengthened:

- All sub-processors must provide evidence of SOC 2 Type II or equivalent certification
- Annual privacy impact assessments are required for any vendor processing more than 10,000 data subject records
- New vendors must complete a privacy questionnaire and security review before contract execution
- The approved sub-processor list will be published and updated quarterly on our Trust Center page

## Training Requirements

All personnel must complete the updated privacy training module by May 20, 2026. The training covers the policy changes above, recognizing and reporting data incidents, and proper handling of data subject requests. Completion rates will be reported to the executive team weekly until 100% is achieved. Failure to complete training by the compliance deadline may result in temporary suspension of access to systems containing personal data.

## Questions and Escalations

Direct all questions about these policy changes to privacy@company.com or the #data-privacy Slack channel. For urgent data incidents, follow the existing incident response procedure and notify the on-call privacy officer within one hour.
